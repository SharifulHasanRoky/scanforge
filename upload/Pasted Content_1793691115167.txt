# 6. STATIC AND DYNAMIC QR CODE SYSTEM

Static and dynamic QR codes must be treated as separate technologies.

## A. STATIC QR CODES

Static QR generation is the primary launch feature.

Requirements:

- Encode actual destination or data directly.
- No redirect server required.
- No database required.
- No account required.
- No watermark.
- No platform-imposed expiry.
- No scan quota.
- No artificial daily generation limit.
- Browser-side generation wherever practical.

Explain that the QR image itself does not expire because of our platform, but its destination or external content may become unavailable.

Static QR destinations cannot be edited after generation.

## B. DYNAMIC QR CODES

Dynamic QR is an advanced feature requiring additional infrastructure.

Potential functionality:

- Editable destination.
- Redirect management.
- Scan counting.
- Scan analytics.
- Device information where legally appropriate.
- Geographic analytics at an appropriate privacy level.
- Campaign management.
- QR code management dashboard.

Dynamic QR requires:

- Backend redirect service.
- Database.
- Secure redirect validation.
- Abuse protection.
- Analytics event storage.
- Monitoring.
- Long-term service reliability.

Do not pretend a static QR code is dynamic.

Do not depend on an external redirect service without disclosing that dependency.

Dynamic QR must not be launched until the backend can reliably maintain the redirect URLs.

Static QR generation must remain free even if dynamic QR infrastructure is introduced.
